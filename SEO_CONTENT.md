## What is Ear Stretching Timeline Planner?

The Ear Stretching Timeline Planner is a free web tool that generates a personalized, step-by-step stretching plan from your current ear gauge size to your target size. The tool uses APP (Association of Professional Piercers) recommended healing intervals to calculate minimum wait times between each gauge increment.

When you select your current gauge and target gauge from dropdown menus (ranging from 18g/1.0mm to 2 inches/50.8mm), the planner instantly displays:
- The total number of stretches required
- Minimum and maximum weeks to reach your goal
- A visual timeline showing each gauge step with healing intervals
- Safe stretching rules based on APP guidelines

The tool's logic validates that your target is larger than your current gauge and calculates wait times based on your current size's millimeter measurement. Smaller gauges require 4-8 weeks between stretches, while larger sizes may need 24-52 weeks.

## Who Should Use This Tool

**Tattoo & Piercing Studio Professionals:** Use the planner to educate clients on safe stretching timelines, provide printed or digital plans during consultations, and establish professional protocols that follow APP guidelines.

**Clients Planning Ear Stretching:** Individuals starting or continuing their ear stretching journey can get realistic timelines, understand the commitment required, and avoid dangerous practices like skipping sizes or stretching too quickly.

**Piercing Apprentices:** Learn proper gauge progression, healing intervals, and how to communicate safe stretching protocols to future clients.

**Studio Managers & Owners:** Standardize stretching consultations across your team and demonstrate your studio's commitment to safety and APP standards.

## How to Use

**Step 1: Select Your Current Gauge/Size**
Open the dropdown menu labeled "Current gauge / size" and choose your current ear piercing size. Options range from 18g (1.0mm) to 2 inches (50.8mm). If you have a standard lobe piercing, 18g or 16g is typical.

**Step 2: Select Your Target Gauge/Size**
Open the dropdown menu labeled "Target gauge / size" and choose your desired final size. The tool defaults to 14g as current and 0g as target, but you can select any combination where the target is larger than the current size.

**Step 3: Click "Plan My Timeline"**
Press the blue "📅 Plan My Timeline" button. The tool validates your selection and generates your personalized plan.

**Step 4: Review Your Results**
Your results display three key statistics at the top:
- Number of stretches required
- Total minimum weeks
- Total months (calculated at 4.33 weeks per month)

Below the statistics, a vertical timeline shows each gauge step with its millimeter measurement and the recommended wait time before progressing to the next size. The starting point and goal are clearly marked.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: What gauge sizes does the Ear Stretching Timeline Planner support?**
The tool supports 21 sizes from 18g (1.0mm) to 2 inches (50.8mm), including all standard gauge sizes and millimeter measurements.

**Q2: Can I plan a stretch from a larger gauge to a smaller gauge?**
No. The tool only generates plans for stretching to larger sizes. If you select a target smaller than your current gauge, the tool displays an error message: "Target must be larger than current gauge."

**Q3: Are the wait times guaranteed to be safe for everyone?**
No. The wait times are APP-recommended minimums for healthy tissue with no complications. Individual healing varies significantly. Scar-prone individuals, those with previous blowouts, or difficult healing history will need longer between each size.

**Q4: What happens if I try to skip a gauge size?**
The tool always generates a complete path through every intermediate gauge. Skipping sizes is dangerous and not recommended by APP guidelines. Each step builds collagen elasticity incrementally.

**Q5: How is the monthly calculation determined?**
The tool divides total weeks by 4.33 (the average number of weeks per month) to provide a realistic monthly estimate.

**Q6: What materials are recommended for safe stretching?**
The tool recommends implant-grade materials only: BioFlex polymer, ASTM F136 titanium, or implant-grade 316LVM steel.

**Q7: What should I do if I feel resistance during stretching?**
If you feel significant resistance during insertion, stop immediately. You are not ready for that size. Never force a taper; use the weight of the jewellery only.

**Q8: Does the tool account for previous blowouts or scar tissue?**
No. The planner provides general APP-guideline intervals based on typical healing tissue. The disclaimer advises consulting a professional piercer if you have a history of blowouts, keloids, or difficult healing.

**Q9: Can I use this tool for other piercings besides ears?**
The tool is specifically designed for ear stretching. The gauge table and wait times are based on ear lobe tissue healing characteristics.

**Q10: How accurate are the millimeter measurements?**
The gauge-to-millimeter conversions follow standard industry measurements. For example, 0g equals 8.0mm, and 25mm equals 1 inch (25.4mm).
